# Amy by Finance BFFs — Design Brief

## Product
Personal finance app for Latin American women who feel overwhelmed by money.
Tone: warm, supportive, zero financial jargon. Like a best friend who happens to know finance.

## Brand
- Product name in UI: **Amy**
- Company: Finance BFFs

## Colors
- Primary / buttons / active states: `#ec7fa9` (hot pink)
- Page background: `#ffedfa` (pale pink)
- Borders / subtle elements: `#ffb8e0` (mid pink)
- Text: `#1a1a2e` (near black)

## Typography
- Headings: Playfair Display (serif, elegant)
- Body: DM Sans (clean, modern)

## Current layout
- Desktop: fixed left sidebar (64px wide) + scrollable main content
- Mobile: sticky top bar with hamburger → slide-in drawer

## Navigation sections
1. Mis finanzas (dashboard)
2. Mis gastos (expense tracking)
3. Cajitas (irregular expense planning)
4. Bolsitas de ahorro (savings pockets)
5. Deudas (debt tracker)

## Core formula shown on dashboard
Dinero libre = Ingresos − Gastos Fijos − Cajitas − Bolsitas − Cuotas Deudas

## Dashboard current structure
- Month selector (top right)
- 3 hero metric cards: Dinero disponible, Total ahorro, Deudas pendientes
- 4 small summary cards: ingresos, gastos fijos, gastos del mes, % disponible
- Detailed sections: Ingresos (editable), Gastos fijos (editable), Gastos del mes, Deudas, Bolsillos

## What we want to improve
- Feels heavy on mobile — too many stacked cards
- Hero metrics should feel more impactful
- Better visual hierarchy: what matters most should stand out
- Keep the warm pink palette but make it feel more premium
- Should feel like a high-end finance app, not a spreadsheet
